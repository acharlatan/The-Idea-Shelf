ABOUT THE IDEA SHELF

Thoughtful marketing.

HOW WE WORK

The source.
The reading.
The next question.

What we cover

Campaigns, customer experiences, packaging, services and brand decisions with a clear mechanism to examine. We look for a specific choice, a reliable source and a practical lesson. Familiarity alone is not a reason to feature a brand.

How we check it

We link to original announcements or documented services, record the year and review date, and retain market and availability limits. Company announcements tell us what the company says; they do not independently establish effectiveness.

How to read a reference

“What happened” summarizes the source. “Our reading” is editorial interpretation. “Try a smaller version” proposes a test. A proposed test is not a case study with measured results.

Illustrations and evidence

Illustrations belong to The Idea Shelf and visualize a theme. They are not photographs of a featured campaign. Original campaign material can be viewed at the linked source.

Results and corrections

We do not invent campaign results or imply interviews took place. The first edition contains no independently measured experiment reports. To suggest a correction, open the repository issue form below with the reference, disputed claim and supporting source. Do not include private customer data.

Suggest a correction →

Publication rhythm

The briefing is designed as a weekly edition with a fixed issue date. Each edition requires editorial review. Older references stay useful through their original dates; they are not presented as breaking news.
